Replace footage with your own licensed clips

In index.html, change a tile's data-media URL to a relative path such as assets/videos/clip.mp4, and place the video at that path. Prefer short, muted 480p clips. Static posters remain available if network access or autoplay is restricted.

ffmpeg -ss 2 -i input.mp4 -t 7 -vf "scale=854:-2,fps=18" -an -c:v libx264 -crf 29 -movflags +faststart clip.mp4
